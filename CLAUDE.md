# Ink Atlas

iPad-first 3D tattoo placement studio. The owner is Jake. He plans tattoos on a 3D body: imports art of existing and planned pieces, places them, compares options, and sends references to his artist.

## History, briefly

1. Jake built v1 through v47 in a ChatGPT thread. It is feature-rich but he found it confusing to use. Live at https://ink-atlas-ipad.jakehalbakken.chatgpt.site. Its full written spec is `docs/v47-spec.md`.
2. Claude rebuilt the core from scratch as a single-page prototype (this repo). Jake's verdict after trying both on his iPad: the rebuild is "a million times better". He wants it to reach full feature parity with v47 and then go past it.
3. The prototype was hosted as a Claude artifact, which blocks the native share sheet, file downloads, network fetches and model loading. This repo exists to get it out of that sandbox.

## How Jake works

- Direct. Wants things done, not proposed. Give opinions, not hedged option lists.
- Tests on a real iPad with touch and Apple Pencil. Nothing counts as working until it works there.
- Builds take hours or a few sessions, not days. Estimate accordingly.
- No em-dashes in anything you write for him.

## What exists today

A Vite app. `src/index.html` is the entry, `src/app.js` the main module (scene, gestures, UI, persistence), with `mesh.js` (shape math), `fit.js` (the list of body sliders), `skin.js` (skin material and anatomy detail), `pose.js` (rig and dual-quaternion skinning), `clothes.js`, `aging.js`, `procreate-edit.js`, `slider.js` (the touch slider), `share.js`, `swatches.js` (Procreate palette), `procreate-draw.js` (guide for drawing a new tattoo on a painted area), `skin-tone.js` (your own skin tone), `lighting.js` (Preview light), `whats-new.js` (the New to try list), `try-on.js` (Try on with camera), `spots.js` (Try other spots), `body-photo.js` and `body-match.js` (Match a photo of me) and `sample-art.js` beside it. `src/body.js` is generated. Builds to `dist/` with three.js and the body data as separate cached chunks, plus a PWA manifest and service worker. Live at https://jakehalbakken.github.io/ink-atlas/ (GitHub Pages, repo `jakehalbakken/ink-atlas`, deploys on push to `main`; Vite `base` is `/ink-atlas/`).

- Canvas-first layout: full-screen body, floating top bar, bottom tray of tattoo thumbnails, editing sheet that appears only when a tattoo is selected. Since the 2026-11-15 organisation pass (`docs/ui-structure.md` is the map every panel follows): one toolbar at the top right (Undo and Redo as icons, New to try, Body, Preview, Plan, Share as the one primary button, then the accounts buttons), the view switcher (Full body, Front, Left, Back, Right) in the top row when it fits and on its own row under it when it doesn't (`placeChrome` in `app.js`, which also sets the CSS `--below` every panel opens under), nothing floating at the left. More controls, Plan and Share are in short headed sections. Colours, radii and type are tokens on `:root` in `index.html`, with a light set under `prefers-color-scheme: light`; the 3D backdrop reads them too (`paintBackdrop`). Use the variables, never fixed colours, in new panels. Headless tests run dark unless `INK_THEME=light`.
- Gesture grammar that never changes by mode: a touch that starts on the selected tattoo moves it; two fingers on it scale and rotate; a touch anywhere else orbits; two fingers elsewhere pinch-zoom and pan (turning the view needs a clear twist, so a two-finger drag is a clean pan). Pencil hover does nothing. Keep this rule. It is the main reason Jake prefers this version.
- Tap a tattoo: select it and keep the view (Jake hates the view jumping); only slide the picture if the edit panel would cover it. A tray chip goes to its tattoo only when it is out of sight; tapping the selected chip again zooms to it. Double-tap empty space or Full body: back to overview.
- Before/after, Pose, Clothes, Aging and Light (Studio, Daylight, Warm room, Midday sun; kept per device in localStorage `ia-light`, never in the project) sit behind one Preview button. Body > Skin tone has a + swatch for your own tone (lighter/deeper and cooler/warmer sliders, or Match a photo of your skin); it is stored in the existing `state.skinHex`, so no storage change. A New to try button (with a count) lists the newest features, each with how to try it and a Show me that opens the panel and rings the control; ticks are kept per device in localStorage `ia-tried` and a feature ticks itself when used. Every batch that ships adds its features to the top of `NEW_FEATURES` in `whats-new.js` in the same deploy. Panels fade while a finger moves the body. Wide landscape puts the tray and edit panel in a right-hand column.
- Ink rendering: decals with multiply blending over a white-backed texture, so ink darkens the lit skin and white paper drops out for free. The decal geometry is a connected-surface walk from the anchor triangle (`src/decal.js`), not three.js DecalGeometry, so ink never jumps to a disconnected surface. Since 2026-11-15 the walk unfolds each triangle flat from the anchor like a paper stencil (isometric unfold across the shared edge, dropping sharp folds and torn or folded-back triangles), so ink keeps its true size along the skin and wraps fully round an arm or leg; the old flat projection cut wide pieces off at the side of the limb. `t.wrap` is still read and saved but changes nothing, and its slider is hidden; Bend and Taper live under "Bend and taper" in the edit sheet.
- Body: MakeHuman CC0 base mesh. Vertex order is body (13,380), eyes (144), then the genital geometry (MakeHuman's helper, subdivided at bake time); body and eye indices must never change because saved anchors use them. Male / neutral / female, MakeHuman's muscle x weight grid (0..1, 0.5 = average), and about 30 fine-tune sliders in `state.fit` (-1..1, 0 = standard) driven by real MakeHuman targets: torso, chest and breasts, arms, legs, neck, genitals. Height is normalised to 1.75 units; a height setting drives real-world size readouts.
- Anatomy detail is painted in the skin shader from landmark vertices, so it follows every shape: eyes, lips, areolae and nipples, navel, crease shading, and muscle definition (abs, chest, back, deltoids, biceps and triceps, quads, kneecaps, calves) as a bump-mapped height field placed from the navel, nipples and the skeleton's joints. More muscle shows more, more weight hides it, the Abs and Back sliders add to it. A posed body keeps its standing positions in a `rest` attribute so the painted detail stays on the same skin in every pose. Genital geometry is on the male body behind a show/hide toggle (Body > Genitals); it is off for every new project, first use and v47 import, and a save keeps its own choice (one without the field, from before, keeps it shown).
- Sliders are the custom `<ink-slider>` element, not `<input type=range>`: it takes horizontal drags and leaves vertical ones to the panel, and the page itself is locked (fixed body plus a touchmove guard). This is the fix for "sliders shift the whole screen".
- Tattoos stay attached through shape changes by anchoring to triangle + barycentric coordinates (`anchorsNow` / `applyAnchors`).
- While a tattoo is dragged, guides show when it lines up with a nearby tattoo's centre or edges (`live-alignment.js`), and a Centre of the body line when it crosses the middle of the chest, stomach, back or neck (`findCentreLine`); with Snap to guides on (More controls > Precise placement) it lands on the line, one undo step.
- Nothing on the body is stuck: every tattoo, locked or already on me, has a bin next to Done (`#bDelTop`, one undo step); paint brought back from Procreate shows as a Paint on skin chip first in the tray (hide, make movable tattoos, remove); Body > Reset to plain body (`resetPlainBody`, under Male / Neutral / Female) clears the paint and keeps every tattoo, one undo step.
- With Reduce Motion on in the device's settings, the view goes straight to a new spot (Full body, a tray chip, Show me) instead of gliding; finger moves are unchanged.
- Undo and Redo say what they put back in a toast (`undo-words.js`: "Undid moving Rose.", "Undid the body shape."), worked out by comparing the saved states either side of the step. Press and hold Undo (or right-click it) for Go back to: the last 15 steps newest first in the same words (`historyList`), and a tap goes back to just before that step in one go (`undoSteps`), every step passed onto Redo.
- On-canvas handles on the selected tattoo: corner resizes, the dot above turns. With a keyboard: arrows move it the way they point on screen (Shift for bigger steps), [ and ] turn it, - and = size it, Cmd+D duplicates, Delete removes, ? lists the keys; presses of one kind close together are one undo step, never while typing in a field.

- Hair (Body > Hair): Bald, Shaved or Buzz cut in five colours, painted on the scalp in the skin shader; Short adds a thin shell (`hair-shell.js`); Medium is about 14,000 strand ribbons (`hair-strands.js`, pure maths) grown inside the hairline over a thin dark base, swept back with height at the front, rebuilt when the shape changes and moved with the skull as one piece in a pose; Long is about 15,000 longer strands, parted in the middle, round the face to just below the shoulders, grown in small steps that keep off the face and slide over the neck and shoulders, with the top moving with the skull and the ends with the upper chest in a pose (blended per vertex in the shader, and pushed out of a neck and jaw capsule where a pose would sink them in). The hairline (`hair.js`, pure maths) comes from the stored face points: high over the forehead, temples, sideburns to mid-ear, a gap round each ear, level at the nape. Kept per device in localStorage `ia-hair`, never in the project. Preview > Clothes still has its own Short/Long hair shell for coverage.
- Beard (Body > Hair): No beard, Stubble, Short beard or Full beard, in the hair colour. `beard.js` (pure maths) gives each head vertex a signed distance into the beard from the stored face points: joins the sideburns, runs down the cheek to the corners of the mouth, takes in the moustache and chin, keeps the lips bare, ends on the neck under the jaw. The skin shader paints stubble and short beard from it; Full adds about 16,000 short curly strands (`buildBeard` in `hair-strands.js`, shown by a second `createHairStrands` with field `beard`) that move with the skull and trim back over a selected tattoo on the face. Kept per device in localStorage `ia-beard`, never in the project.
- Body hair (Body > Hair): No body hair, Light or Full, in the hair colour. `body-hair.js` (pure maths) gives each body vertex a density and the direction hair lies from the skeleton's joints and the nipples and navel: forearms and lower legs most, the front and outside of the thighs, a little on the outside of the upper arms, the middle of the chest and a line down from the navel; hands, back, shoulders and face stay bare, and a female body gets far less. The skin shader paints fine hairs along that direction close up and a soft textured tint further away. Worked out only while it shows. Kept per device in localStorage `ia-body-hair`, never in the project.
- Your face on the body (Body > Add my face): MediaPipe's face landmarker (`public/vision/face_landmarker.task`, Apache-2.0) finds 478 points in a selfie on the device. The same points were found once on the 3D head and stored as triangle + barycentric weights (`src/face-map.js`, made by `tools/face-map-bake.mjs`), so they follow every shape. `face-fit.js` (pure maths) pulls the head's front towards the photo's face shape (display only, added in `setBodyPositions`, never saved) and gives each head vertex a place in the photo; the skin shader lays on the photo's detail (photo divided by a soft blur of the face) over the body's own tone, and the irises take the photo's colour. Offers the photo's skin tone as one undoable step. The cropped photo and its points live in localStorage `ia-face`, per device, never in the project or backups. The selfie's landmark depth, less the plain head's (`src/face-depth.js`, made by `tools/face-depth-bake.mjs`), shapes the head front to back too. Hair up to Long, to just below the shoulders (Body > Hair, with Match my hair from a photo for its colour and length).

## First tasks, in order

Status: all four done and confirmed by Jake on the iPad (2026-11-10). Kept here for the record.

1. `npm install`, `npm run build`, open `dist/index.html`, confirm it runs.
