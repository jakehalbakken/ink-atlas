      Paint on skin

      What you painted on the 3D body in Procreate lies on the skin as one layer. It is not a tattoo yet, so it can't be moved or deleted on its own. Your tattoos are not part of it.

      Aging

      See how your tattoos may look as they age. Lines soften and spread a little, and black ink lightens. A rough guide, not a promise. Only for looking, not saved.

      Very fine lines and small lettering blur first. If a design still reads at 10 years, it will hold up.
